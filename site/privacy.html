Privacy policy

What this site does and does not do with your data.

Last updated 2026-09-24.

This policy explains what happens to your data when you visit vpsticker.com. It is short, because this site does very little with it.

What this site collects directly

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No forms. There are no contact forms, comment boxes, or newsletter sign-ups on this site.
  • No cookies set by this site. We do not set cookies, and we do not use local storage or anything similar.
  • No analytics or tracking of our own. We do not run our own analytics script, tag manager, or session recorder on this site.
  • Third-party advertising. This site displays ads from third-party ad networks. Those networks may set their own cookies and collect data (such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited) under their own privacy policies. You can usually control or disable advertising cookies in your browser settings or through the ad network's opt-out page.

What ad networks may collect

When you visit a page that shows an ad, the ad network's script runs in your browser. Depending on the network, it may collect:

  • Your IP address and approximate location.
  • Your browser type, screen size, and operating system.
  • Which pages you viewed on this site.
  • A cookie or similar identifier so the network can remember your preferences or limit how often you see the same ad.

We do not control what the ad network collects — that is governed by the network's own privacy policy. What we can promise is that ad placement never changes which deals are listed or how they rank. Ranking is always by published price.

Third-party vendors and advertising cookies

Ads on this site are served by third-party advertising vendors. Those vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites. Third-party vendors and ad networks may also use advertising identifiers to measure how ads perform.

You can opt out of personalised advertising from participating vendors through the industry opt-out pages below. Opting out does not remove ads from the page — it means the ads you see are no longer tailored using that data:

  • aboutads.info/choices (Digital Advertising Alliance)
  • youronlinechoices.com (European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance)
  • optout.networkadvertising.org (Network Advertising Initiative)

This site does not control those opt-out tools and cannot act on your behalf through them. Browser-level controls also work: most browsers let you block or clear third-party cookies entirely in their settings.

Which networks are active right now. No advertising network is currently serving ads on this site. The ad code is added by hand, after a network approves the site, and the network's identity is named here as soon as that happens. Until then, this page describes what will apply rather than what is running.

What the hosting provider sees

This site is a set of static files served by Cloudflare. Like any web host or content delivery network, Cloudflare processes request data — such as your IP address, user agent, and the page requested — in order to deliver the page and protect the site from abuse. That processing is carried out by Cloudflare under Cloudflare's own privacy policy. We do not run a separate analytics system on top of it.

Outbound links

Links to hosting providers take you to someone else's website. Once you are there, that provider's own privacy and cookie policies apply — not this one. Some of those links may be affiliate links, meaning a commission may be paid to us if you sign up. Affiliate status never affects what is listed or how it ranks.

Email

If you email us, we keep your message and your address so we can reply. We do not add you to any mailing list, and we do not sell or share your address. See the contact page.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data.
  • We do not build visitor profiles.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data to this site, use the contact page and it will be removed.

Your agreement to this policy

By using this site, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree with it, the appropriate step is to stop using the site. Because this site sets no cookies of its own and runs no first-party analytics, there is no account to close and no profile to delete — there is nothing held about you to remove. If a third-party ad network has set a cookie in your browser, the opt-out links above and your browser's own settings are the way to remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Because the site's behaviour is fixed by its build, this page describes what the site actually does at that date rather than what it is expected to do later. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated version; if you want to check what changed, the site's source history is public.

vpsticker is an independent price radar. It is not affiliated with the providers listed. Prices are read from each provider's own public pages and are shown as published; always confirm on the provider's site before buying. Some outbound links may be affiliate links.
