About vpsticker

What this site is, how the prices are collected, and what it refuses to do.

Last updated 2026-09-18.

vpsticker is an independent VPS price radar. It reads the prices that hosting providers publish on their own public pricing pages, and rebuilds this site from them every six hours.

Who runs this

vpsticker is run by one independent operator, a side project rather than a company.

It is not a hosting company and does not sell hosting. It has no editorial team, no sponsored content, and no relationship with the providers beyond reading their public pricing pages and, where one exists, an affiliate link.

Corrections, complaints and takedown requests all go to the same place: the contact page. If a price here is wrong, that is a bug, and it gets fixed by fixing the extraction, not by editing the number.

How it works

A scheduled job fetches each provider's public pricing page, extracts the prices that are actually published there, and writes them into this site. There is no database, no account, and no hand-entered data.

Every offer links back to the exact page it was read from. The Sources page shows, provider by provider, the HTTP result, how the price was extracted, and how many offers were found on the last run — including the providers that produced nothing.

What this site does not do

  • It does not estimate, average, or back-fill a missing price. If a provider does not publish a machine-readable price, the offer is listed without a price.
  • It does not bypass bot protection, log in anywhere, or read anything that is not a public page.
  • It does not accept payment for placement. Ordering is by published price only — nobody can pay to move up.
  • It is not affiliated with any of the providers listed.

Affiliate disclosure

Some outbound links on this site may be affiliate links. If you sign up through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate status never affects whether a provider is listed, where it ranks, or what price is shown — the numbers are read from the providers' own pages either way.

Why a price here may differ from what you see

Prices change. A price shown here was true on the provider's own page at the timestamp printed on the offer. Always confirm on the provider's site before buying.
